THE NAT GALA, 2025

A 10-Year Cultural Movement for Nature

Not a moment. A movement.

THE NAT was built to close the gap between culture and conservation — turning one extraordinary night into lasting capital and attention for the natural world.

In our epic first year, THE NAT welcomed Harrison Ford, Stella McCartney, Billie Eilish, Sabrina Elba, Hindou Ibrahim, Jane Fonda, Peter Seligmann, Sophie Hunter, Nemonte Nenquimo and Van Jones, among many others.

Why We Gather — 01

Support Nature

Nature funds itself last. Of the roughly $711 billion needed each year to protect the natural world, only a fraction ever arrives. THE NAT is here to accelerate that change — closing the gap is our long-term mission, and THE NAT GALA is one night in that pursuit, turning a single evening into meaningful capital for the planet.

$3M 2026 goal for nature · $2M raised in year one · 340 gathered in 2025.

All funds are payable to THE NAT FUND Inc., a registered 501(c)(3) organization. Profits go direct to our beneficiaries.
