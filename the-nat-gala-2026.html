A Night in the Current

A sensory journey through the full cycle of water — built in light, sound and taste.

  1. 01

    Salt of The Earth

    Sophie Hunter’s salt marsh performance, adapted from Venice for New York.

  2. 02

    Dinner by Yann Nury

    Every ingredient sourced within 100 miles of the venue.

  3. 03

    Two films by Studio Prince

    Thomas Crowther on the science of optimism. Dr. Sylvia Earle in correspondence with the deep.

  4. 04

    Water Is Life

    Dee Jay Two Bears brought Fancy Dance and popping together to mark ten years since Standing Rock.

  5. 05

    Leading Voices

    Inspirational words on stage from foremost thinkers in science, investment, business and ocean. Thank you Thomas Crowther, Emma Watson, Tenzin Seldon, Jesper Brodin, Lord Fakafānua (Prime Minister of Tonga) and Titouan Bernicot.

  6. 06

    NATCAP: Coral Rave

    KP The Great below ground, with Spotify and Coral Gardeners — funding THE NAT GARDEN coral nursery in Tahiti.

A 10-Year Cultural Movement for Nature

Not a moment. A movement.

THE NAT was built to close the gap between culture and conservation — turning one extraordinary night into lasting capital and attention for the natural world.

In our epic first year, THE NAT welcomed Harrison Ford, Stella McCartney, Billie Eilish, Sabrina Elba, Hindou Ibrahim, Jane Fonda, Peter Seligmann, Sophie Hunter, Nemonte Nenquimo and Van Jones, among many others.
