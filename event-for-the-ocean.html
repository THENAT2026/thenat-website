Thursday 24 September 2026

For the Ocean

Sport, culture and the changing ocean — the closing night of Climate Week NYC

WSA, Water Street, New York

THE NAT

Event Summary

An evening for the ocean

On the closing night of Climate Week NYC 2026, THE NAT and its co-hosts brought the week’s ocean community together at WSA on Water Street, at the water’s edge in Lower Manhattan.

Rather than one more conference session, it was designed as an evening: a reception, a candid panel on sport, culture and the ocean, and live music — looking ahead to Oceans 2026 in Western Australia this December.

The Central Question

How can we use the culture of sport to protect the natural world?

Four Themes from the Night

  1. 01

    The ocean has never been more on the agenda.

    Guests described a Climate Week where ocean and nature finance moved to the centre of the conversation.

  2. 02

    Culture moves people where panels can’t.

    Sport, art and music reach people from the heart — and that is what builds a movement.

  3. 03

    Unlikely partnerships are the engine.

    Athletes, investors, artists and scientists in one room is the model — and it is only the beginning.

  4. 04

    Capital needs a reason to care.

    Investors are working out how to make ocean solutions pay. What THE NAT adds, in one guest’s words, is “the beauty and the hope.”
